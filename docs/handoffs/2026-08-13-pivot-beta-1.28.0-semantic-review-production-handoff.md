## Production activation contract

- `PIVOT_AXIS_SEMANTIC_REVIEW=1` explicitly enables composition.
- `PIVOT_AXIS_CORRECTNESS_REVIEW_MODEL` is required and must differ from the
  active Worker model.
- `PIVOT_AXIS_SECURITY_REVIEW_MODEL` is optional; when present it must also be
  distinct from both the Worker and correctness Reviewer models.
- Route budgets are code-owned. Provider configuration and secrets remain in
  Main. The Renderer and Worker receive no administrative or filesystem Port.
- Missing, malformed, over-budget, timed-out, circuit-open or rejected review
  evidence fails closed before durable Guarded Safe Write completion.

## Important limits

- Semantic Review remains default-off and requires explicit deployment model
  identifiers; it is not automatically enabled by choosing a Provider.
- No production fallback Reviewer pool exists. Circuit-open and provider
  failure deliberately escalate to human review.
- Large diff chunking, richer line mapping, review telemetry/UI, realistic
  provider field validation and long-run recovery/chaos campaigns remain.
- The product-package Obsidian baseline was not changed in this slice set.
