## React Bits — SpotlightCard-derived interaction

Pivot's `SpotlightSurface` interaction is adapted from the SpotlightCard concept in [React Bits](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits). The implementation has been rewritten for Pivot's design tokens, pointer-event model, accessibility requirements, and animation budget.
